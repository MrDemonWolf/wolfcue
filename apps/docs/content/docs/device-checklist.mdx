---
title: Moblin device checklist
description: Verify recording, scrolling, export, and saving on your phone.
---

These checks require a physical iPhone and owner review. No item is marked
passed by an automated test. Use synthetic text for the first run.

1. Open Moblin's private browser, not a Browser scene widget, and load
   `https://wolfcue.mrdemonwolf.dev` in the large view after deployment. Also
   try the **Set up WolfCue in Moblin** link from the Moblin guide in Safari
   and note whether Moblin turns on its Browser button and opens WolfCue.
2. Paste synthetic text. Confirm **Saved on this device** appears.
3. Set pace and text size. Switch to the small view. Confirm Pause, time, and
   Settings are visible and nothing essential sits under Moblin's expand button.
4. In the small view, compare text sizes 28, 32, and 36 px from your usual
   camera distance. Note which reads best.
5. Start a local recording, not a live stream. Start reading with the countdown.
6. Read for 60 seconds. Pause and resume at least twice. Check smooth scrolling,
   confirm there are no jumps, and note whether the reading line near the top
   looks natural on camera.
7. Leave the phone untouched past its Auto-Lock time with **Keep screen awake**
   on. Record whether the screen stays awake.
8. Toggle the browser between small and large mid-read. Check the reading
   position and note whether playback pauses.
9. Hide and re-show the browser. Confirm it is paused on return and does not leap.
10. Rotate the phone. Confirm reading position and layout hold.
11. Stop recording. Review saved video and audio for interruptions and confirm
    the script does not appear in the recording.
12. Try Import and each Export option, including whether **Download .txt** does
    anything in Moblin. Confirm a backup actually saved.
13. Open a script link, then force-quit Moblin and reopen its browser. Check
    whether script and settings remain saved, and whether Moblin reopens the
    link. Note whether the link appears in Safari or Moblin history.
14. Repeat steps 2 to 6 in Safari and confirm its script storage is separate.

Also note the iPhone model, iOS version, Moblin version, Low Power Mode state,
and which browser size/orientation you used. Do not paste private scripts into
bug reports or screenshots.
