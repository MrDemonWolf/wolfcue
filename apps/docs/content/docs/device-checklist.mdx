---
title: Moblin device checklist
description: Verify recording, scrolling, export, and saving on your phone.
---

These checks require a physical iPhone and owner review. No item is marked
passed by an automated test. Use synthetic text for the first run.

1. Open Moblin's private browser, not a Browser scene widget, and load
   `https://wolfcue.mrdemonwolf.dev` in the large view after deployment.
2. Paste synthetic text. Confirm **Saved on this device** appears.
3. Set pace and text size. Switch to the small view. Confirm Pause, time, and
   Settings are visible and nothing essential sits under Moblin's expand button.
4. Start a local recording, not a live stream. Start reading with the countdown.
5. Read for 60 seconds. Pause and resume at least twice. Check smooth scrolling
   and confirm there are no jumps.
6. Leave the phone untouched past its Auto-Lock time with **Keep screen awake**
   on. Record whether the screen stays awake.
7. Toggle the browser between small and large mid-read. Check the reading
   position and note whether playback pauses.
8. Hide and re-show the browser. Confirm it is paused on return and does not leap.
9. Rotate the phone. Confirm reading position and layout hold.
10. Stop recording. Review saved video and audio for interruptions and confirm
    the script does not appear in the recording.
11. Try Import and each Export option. Note which options work in Moblin and
    confirm a backup actually saved.
12. Force-quit Moblin and reopen its browser. Check whether script and settings
    remain saved.
13. Repeat steps 2 to 5 in Safari and confirm its script storage is separate.

Also note the iPhone model, iOS version, Moblin version, Low Power Mode state,
and which browser size/orientation you used. Do not paste private scripts into
bug reports or screenshots.
