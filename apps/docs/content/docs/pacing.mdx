---
title: Pacing
description: Choose words per minute or an approximate scroll completion time.
---

## Words per minute

Choose 60 to 300 words per minute, in steps of 5. The default is 150. WolfCue
measures the rendered script height and word count to calculate pixels per
second. The editor estimate is word count divided by your chosen pace.
Changing text size or viewport size recalculates the scroll speed to preserve
that word rate.

## Target time

Choose 10 seconds to 60 minutes, in steps of 5 seconds. The default is 60
seconds. This is the approximate time when scrolling reaches the end. It does
not measure speech, pauses in your delivery, or how many words you actually read.

Speed is recalculated when you resume, seek, change pace, or change layout.
It uses the remaining distance and target time after active reading time.
Paused and countdown time do not count. Seeking keeps the elapsed timer.

To avoid an abrupt rush, target mode is capped at the speed corresponding to
300 wpm. If the requested target needs a faster rate, WolfCue shows that the
target will be exceeded and reports remaining time at the actual capped rate.

Use a short rehearsal to find a comfortable pace.
